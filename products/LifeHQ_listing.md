## Full description
**What you get**
- **Budget tab** — set a budget per category; spending flows in automatically from your expense log; see Remaining per category and a monthly total.
- **Expenses tab** — a clean dropdown-driven log (date, category, description, amount).
- **Savings goals** — targets with automatic % complete.
- **Habits tab** — a full 31-day grid; tick a cell to mark a habit done; get "Done" count and % of month per habit.
- **Dashboard** — income, spent, left to spend, % of budget used, savings progress, habit completion %, best habit, total check-ins, and your top 3 spending categories — all live, nothing to fill in.
- **Fully customisable** — edit your categories and habits on the Setup tab; everything follows.
- **Any currency** — no fixed symbol.

**Who it's for**
Anyone who wants to get a grip on both their spending and their routines without paying for two apps. Students, freelancers, anyone doing a monthly reset.

**How to use it**
Download → open in Excel or import to Google Sheets → set your budget, log as you go. Duplicate the Expenses/Habits tabs each month for a fresh start.
