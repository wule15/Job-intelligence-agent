## Full description
**What you get**
- **Game Library** — one row per game: title, platform, genre, status, ownership (physical/digital/subscription), rating, hours, price paid, cost-per-hour, date beaten, and a personal review field. All dropdown-driven.
- **Live Dashboard** — total games, hours played, money spent, average rating, completion %, and a count of every status. Nothing to fill in; it updates as you log.
- **Pile of Shame counter** — the honest number: how many games are sitting in your backlog.
- **Best Value leaderboard** — your games auto-ranked by cost-per-hour, so you can see which ones were actually worth it.
- **What to Play Next** — a random pick from your backlog to end decision paralysis.
- **Fully customisable** — edit platforms, genres and statuses on the Lists tab; dropdowns follow.
- **Any currency** — Price Paid has no fixed symbol.

**Who it's for**
Collectors, completionists, and anyone with a backlog they'll never admit the size of. If you want a beautiful, private, spreadsheet you fully control — instead of another app account — this is it.

**Honest note**
