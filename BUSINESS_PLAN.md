# Digital Business Plan — Nikola Vukadin

*Bootstrap side business → scale to replace day job. 10–20 hrs/week, near-zero start cost, zero audience.*

---

## The one truth this plan is built around

The product is the easy part. With **zero audience and zero budget, distribution is the entire game.**
A generic Notion template or PDF ebook is a lottery ticket — 50,000 exist and the winners already had followings.

Your unfair advantage is **not** "can make a template." It is:

- **Technical credibility** — a real engineer who specs equipment, not a content generalist.
- **AI-search / SEO / GEO skill** — you can make a product get *found* on Google and cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity. That is free, compounding distribution most template-sellers cannot buy.
- **You can build automations** — Python, Sheets, APIs. You can ship tools, not just documents.

Every idea below is chosen so **buyers find you** instead of you needing an audience. That means: niches with **evergreen search demand**, **buyers who have money** (professionals/SMBs, not students or broke job-seekers), and **low competition from creators** (blue-collar/industrial, which the Notion-template crowd ignores).

---

## Positioning wedge

**Operational digital products for SMBs that run physical operations** — maintenance, fleet, trucking/dispatch, field service — plus **AI-visibility** for B2B/industrial companies.

Why this wins for *you specifically*:
- You have **genuine operational credibility** here (194-machine preventive-maintenance system, valve asset surveys, trucking dispatch + recruiting Excel tools). This is not a niche you're faking.
- Almost **no digital-product sellers** target it — they all sell productivity/creator/finance templates. You'd be one of very few, so search competition is thin.
- These buyers **have budget** and hate their current spreadsheets. Willingness to pay is real.
- It does **not** require deep mech-eng from the buyer's side — it's simple, digestible, drag-and-drop, exactly the "easy to use" thing you want.

## 1) Digital products (the core of what you asked for)

Ranked. Build **#1 first.**

### ⭐ #1 — "Fleet & Maintenance Command Center" (Notion + Google Sheets template) — RECOMMENDED
A done-for-you operational system for small fleet / maintenance / construction-equipment owners: machine register, service history, preventive-maintenance triggers (engine hours / distance), spare-parts tracking, permit/inspection expiry alerts, cost-per-machine dashboard.
- **You've already built this** at Galax — you're productizing real work, not inventing.
- Two versions: **Google Sheets** (blue-collar buyers live in Sheets, not Notion) and **Notion** (for the tidier operators).
- Price: **$39–$79** one-time. Add a "Pro" tier with automation (Apps Script alerts) at **$129**.
- Build time: ~15–25 hrs for v1. Fits 1–2 weeks of your hours.

### #2 — "Dispatch & Driver Recruiting Kit" for small trucking companies
Sheets/Notion system for load/dispatch tracking + a driver-recruiting pipeline (you already built both for the trucking client). Trucking is a huge, underserved, cash-heavy US market that buys tools.
- Natural **collaboration with your friend's company** — his real usage = testimonials, case study, and a warm first channel.
- Price: **$49–$99**. Strong upsell path to a subscription tool later (see SaaS).

### #3 — "AI Visibility DIY Toolkit" (PDF playbook + Sheets scorecard + prompt pack)
Productize the audit methodology you already published (Emerson/Fisher, 17-company sector study). Buyer runs your crawl-readiness + citation test on their own site.
- Buyers: B2B/industrial marketing managers. **Higher price, $99–$199**, because it's tied to revenue.
- Doubles as a **lead magnet** for the audit *service* (below) — every buyer is a warm services lead.

### #4 — Engineering micro-tools as products (later, low priority)
Valve-sizing cheat-sheets, Cv/Kv calculators (Google Sheets), P&ID/ATEX quick-reference PDFs. Evergreen search demand, but **low willingness to pay** (students/individual engineers). Good as **free SEO magnets** that funnel to #1–#3, not as primary revenue.

## 2) Services (start these WEEK ONE — they fund the products and create proof)

Services give you cash and case studies in *weeks*, need *zero audience* (cold outreach + SEO), and every engagement hands you a product idea and a testimonial.

### ⭐ Productized service: "AI Visibility Audit" — $500–$1,500 per audit
You've already done this publicly for named companies. Package it: fixed scope, fixed price, 3–5 day turnaround, standard deliverable. Sell to B2B/industrial companies via cold LinkedIn/email (your Emerson & sector studies are the portfolio). **This is your fastest path to first revenue.**

### Technical content / SEO retainer — $600–$2,000/mo
Exactly your portfolio pitch (Tameson case study). One or two retained industrial clients = meaningful monthly income while products compound in the background.

**Rule:** services buy your runway; products are the asset that eventually scales past your hours.

## 3) SaaS (the scale path — only AFTER a product validates the pain)

Don't start here. Start with the template; when buyers keep asking "can it just *do* this automatically," that's your signal to build the tool.

### ⭐ "SimpleFleet" — a lightweight maintenance/fleet tracker
The natural evolution of Product #1. Small fleet/construction/maintenance operators, priced **$15–$49/mo**. Preventive-maintenance triggers, service log, parts, expiry alerts, one clean dashboard. Competitors (Fiix, UpKeep) are enterprise-heavy and expensive — there's room at the small, cheap, dead-simple end.
- Buildable solo with Supabase + a simple frontend, or even a paid Notion/Sheets + automation layer at first.
- Your template buyers are the pre-validated launch list.

### Secondary: "DispatchLite" for small carriers
Same logic, evolved from Product #2, with your friend's company as design partner #1.

**SaaS test:** only build it once a template in the same niche has paying customers asking for automation. That de-risks months of engineering.

---

## Why this order (template → service → SaaS)

- **Service** = cash now, no audience needed, builds authority.
- **Template** = your first scalable asset, low build cost, validates the niche and collects buyers.
- **SaaS** = the scale engine, built only after real buyers prove the pain and become your launch list.

Each stage de-risks and funds the next. Nothing here needs ad spend or a following to start.

## Distribution plan (this is the real work — do not skip)

With zero audience, sales come from **being found**, not from posting to nobody. Priorities:

1. **SEO / programmatic pages** — publish a buying/comparison/how-to page for each product ("preventive maintenance schedule template for construction equipment"). This is *your* skill; use it on yourself. Free, compounding.
2. **AI-search optimization (GEO)** — structure every product page for citation by ChatGPT/Perplexity. Almost no competitor in these niches does this. Direct edge.
3. **Marketplaces for cold demand** — list templates on Gumroad + Notion's template gallery + Etsy (Sheets templates sell surprisingly well there). Built-in buyer traffic, zero audience required.
4. **Cold outreach for services** — 10 targeted LinkedIn/email touches a day to industrial companies with weak AI visibility (you can literally screenshot their failing citation test as the hook).
5. **Niche communities** — fleet/trucking/maintenance subreddits, Facebook groups, forums. Answer questions, link the free tool. Blue-collar niches are under-served here.
6. **Build LinkedIn as a byproduct** — post each audit finding / template build. 150 connections is fine to start; consistency compounds. Not the main channel, just free reinforcement.

## First 90 days

**Weeks 1–2**
- Package the **AI Visibility Audit** service (scope, price, one-page sales doc). Start 10 cold touches/day.
- Outline **Product #1** (Fleet & Maintenance Command Center); decide Sheets-first.

**Weeks 3–4**
- Build Product #1 v1 (Sheets). Publish a Gumroad page + one SEO article. List on Etsy/Notion gallery.
- Land **first paid audit** (target: 1–2).

**Weeks 5–8**
- Ship the Notion version + Pro (automation) tier of Product #1.
- Start **Product #2 (Dispatch/Recruiting Kit)** with your friend's company as first user + testimonial.
- Publish 2–3 more SEO/GEO pages. Keep outreach running.

**Weeks 9–12**
- Launch Product #2. Bundle #1 + #2.
- Ship the **AI Visibility DIY Toolkit** (#3) as a product *and* a lead magnet feeding the audit service.
- Review numbers: which product has traction + are buyers asking for automation? → decide whether to greenlight **SimpleFleet SaaS**.

**Success checkpoint at day 90:** at least one paying audit client + first template sales + a validated niche. That's the trigger to lean harder and start the SaaS.
